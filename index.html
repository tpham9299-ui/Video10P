Video10P Studio

Automated workflow for producing and publishing educational mathematics storytelling videos to TikTok.

What This Application Does

Video10P Studio enables creators to render animated math history lessons and upload approved video content directly to their authorized TikTok accounts via TikTok Content Posting API.

How TikTok Integration Works

  • Creators authenticate explicitly using TikTok OAuth 2.0.
  • Videos are uploaded only when initiated by the authorized account owner.
  • Creators retain full control to edit captions, privacy settings, or revoke access at any time.
